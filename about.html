ABOUT FORCE MOTORS

INDIA’S LARGEST VAN MAKER

Force Motors was founded in 1958 by Shri Navalmal Kundanmal Firodia, with a vision of contributing to modern India through industrialisation and making transportation affordable, reliable and efficient. Today, under the leadership of Dr. Abhay Firodia, Force Motors operates as a fully integrated automobile company with capabilities spanning engineering, development and manufacturing of vehicles, aggregates and components.

WHAT FORCE MOTORS DOES

MOBILITY FOR EVERY JOURNEY

Force Motors develops mobility products ranging from light commercial vehicles and multi-utility vehicles to specialised vehicles. Its vehicle portfolio includes Traveller N, Urbania DX, Monobus, Trax, Special Applications, Gurkha and electric mobility products.

MANUFACTURING & ENGINEERING STRENGTH

ENGINEERED IN INDIA. BUILT FOR THE JOURNEY.

Force Motors operates five advanced manufacturing facilities in India (Akurdi, Chakan, Pithampur, and Chennai). Drawing on Indo-German technological collaboration, automation, precision engineering, and Industry 4.0 adoption.

FROM THE CHAIRMAN'S DESK

BUILDING NATIONWIDE MOBILITY FOR INDIA

Force Motors' origins are deeply connected with nation-building and grassroots mobility. From the early development of India's autorickshaw ecosystem to iconic mobility platforms such as Traveller and Trax, Force Motors has consistently focused on providing reliable transport for India.

Today, with modern products such as Urbania and key contributions to defence mobility through Gurkha platforms, Force Motors continues to push Indian engineering onto the global stage.
