DEFINITIONS

SECTION 02

AWARDS OBJECTIVE

The Travel Awards objective is to recognize, honor, and encourage excellence across the Indian travel, hospitality, tourism, and mobility ecosystem—setting benchmarks for service standards, innovation, infrastructure, and operational reliability.

SECTION 03

AWARD SEGMENTS & CATEGORIES

The awards programme features 5 core segments: Tourist Attraction / Adventure Location Enablers, Marketing Excellence, Hospitality, Travel Enablers, and Travel Agents & Operators. Explore Categories →

SECTION 04

ELIGIBILITY PARAMETERS

Participating organizations must have a minimum registered presence in India for at least two years as of December 31, 2025 (). Eligible project implementation period is (). All entries must concern fully implemented commercial or operational initiatives rather than purely conceptual work. One organization may enter multiple categories provided separate applications are submitted for distinct initiatives.

SECTION 05

COMPLETENESS & DISQUALIFICATION

All nomination forms must be completed in English. Submitting false, misleading, or materially inaccurate information will lead to immediate disqualification. The Jury and Awards Management reserve the right to audit claims or request additional verification documents.

SECTION 07

RECEIPT OF ENTRIES

All nomination entries must be submitted prior to the entry deadline: (). Late submissions are subject strictly to approved Awards Management discretion.

SECTION 08

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION & AUDIT

Awards Management or the independent screening panel may contact entrants for clarification, supplemental financial/operational metrics, or supporting evidence. Failure to supply requested information within specified timelines may result in entry disqualification.

SECTION 09

DETERMINATION OF WINNERS

Finalists and winners are determined based on predefined scoring parameters evaluated by the independent Grand Jury. Awards may be withheld in any category if no entry meets the required jury benchmark.

SECTION 10

GENERAL CONDITIONS & INDEMNITY

Decisions of the Jury and Awards Management are final, binding, and non-contestable. Awards Management reserves the right to modify categories, evaluation timelines, or event dates as necessary. All submitted non-confidential media materials may be used by Awards Management for promotional and coverage purposes.
